Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Banglo Hitam ("we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use this website and your rights, in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

1. Information we collect

This website has no accounts, logins, forms or online ordering, and we do not ask you to provide personal data on it. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers. Our hosting provider and other services needed to load the page (such as Google Fonts) may automatically receive technical data such as IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed and time of visit.

2. Information you give us elsewhere

If you message us on Instagram or TikTok or contact us about a booking, we may receive your name, username and message content. We use it only to reply to you, handle your enquiry or booking, and improve our service.

3. Third-party services

Links to Google Maps, Instagram and TikTok take you to services operated by others. They collect and use your data under their own privacy policies, which we do not control. Please review them before use.

4. How we use and share information

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only with service providers who help run our business, when you ask us to, or when required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

5. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal data, including serving this website over a secure HTTPS connection and not collecting unnecessary data. No internet transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will take appropriate action and notify you where the law requires.

6. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, then delete or anonymise it.

7. International transfers

Some third-party providers may process data outside Malaysia. By using the website you understand this may occur under their safeguards.

8. Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Under the PDPA you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or withdraw consent, by messaging @banglohitam on Instagram. We may need to verify your identity first.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or complaints, message us on Instagram: @banglohitam.
