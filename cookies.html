Cookies Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how cookies and similar technologies relate to the Banglo Hitam website.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels.

2. Our use of cookies

This website does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. We do not use cookies to profile you.

3. Technical data and fonts

To display the page, your browser connects to our hosting provider and to Google Fonts, which may receive technical data such as your IP address. This is needed for the site to work.

4. Third-party cookies

When you follow links to Google Maps, Instagram or TikTok, those services may set their own cookies and track activity under their own policies. We do not control them. Please check: Google (policies.google.com), Instagram (help.instagram.com) and TikTok (tiktok.com/legal).

5. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use private browsing. Doing so will not stop this website from working.

6. Changes

If we add analytics or other cookies in future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

7. Contact

Questions? Message us on Instagram: @banglohitam.
