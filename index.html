Why Banglo Hitam

At Banglo Hitam, every detail is designed to help you slow down and feel at home. Surrounded by a cool, refreshing breeze and an effortlessly aesthetic setting, we offer a calm, relaxed escape from the noise of the city. Pull up a chair, soak in the peaceful atmosphere, and treat yourself to the finest coffee, handcrafted drinks, and mouthwatering food and all served in a space you will want to linger in.
